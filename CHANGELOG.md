# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Logs every fished item per character, per fishing session and per subzone.
- Catch window while a fishing pole is in hand: bars per item with count and share, for this
  session, this character or all characters; the subzone or the whole zone.
- Item tooltip: where the item was caught, best share first.
- Window position, size and opacity in Edit Mode, per layout; see-through by default.
- Text settings in the options and in Edit Mode: any LibSharedMedia font, size, outline, high
  quality (Slug) rendering, title and text colour, items in their quality's colour, and a shadow
  with its own colour and x/y offset.
- Bar settings after ElvUI's health bar: texture, colour, colour by share, background colour or
  the bar's, background multiplier, transparent, invert, reverse fill and smooth bars. By default
  light blue on a half-dark background, which stands out on water.
- `/fcv` commands and options in the game's AddOns settings.
- Day and night, and seasons: the item tooltip says when a fish seems to bite only then.
- "New here!" for an item's first catch at a place, and a kinds counter in the window.
- Records: biggest stack per item, most catches in a session, longest session.
- A data broker object with the session's catches, the rarest catch here and the records.
- Sharing catch counts with the guild, with a Guild view in the window.
